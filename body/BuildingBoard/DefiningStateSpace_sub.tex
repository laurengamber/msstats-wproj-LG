The Monopoly game board has 40 landable spaces, so the state space is $S = s_1, s_2 \ldots, s_{40}$. The spaces are numbered in the order that the player moves around the board, where $s_1$ is the GO space and $s_{40}$ is the Boardwalk property. Each time step $t$ is one full turn of a player's movement. As such, the state $X_t$ is the space the player occupies at the end of turn $t$. This is a discrete-time Markov chain with a finite state space. It is also time-homogeneous because the dice and game rules are the same each turn.
