A player is out of the game once they declare bankruptcy. If a player owes more Monopoly money than they have, they must try to raise money before they can declare bankruptcy. A player can raise money by selling buildings. Players can sell hotels to the bank for half price and exchange them for 4 houses. Players can also sell houses to the bank for half price. Houses must be sold evenly across the color set. Once all houses are sold in a color set, properties can be mortgaged, meaning they are sold to the bank for half their cost. Properties can be unmortgaged if a player, in future turns, pays the bank the unmortgaged cost on the deed card. Rent cannot be collected on mortgaged properties.

If a player cannot raise sufficient funds to pay their debts, that player is out of the game and must give all mortgaged properties and any Get Out of Jail Free cards to the player they owe.
