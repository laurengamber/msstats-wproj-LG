



The rules in this section follow the instructions Hasbro published for the U.S. edition of Monopoly \citep{hasbro_monopoly_rules}.
In Monopoly, players move around the board buying and building on properties to collect more rent and become the wealthiest player. The game ends when all but one player has gone bankrupt. All currency in the game is Monopoly money, denoted by M with two horizontal bars, which this paper writes as \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}\citep{fields2016monopoly}.
