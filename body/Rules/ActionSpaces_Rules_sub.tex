When a player lands on the following spaces, they follow these instructions. On Go, the player collects \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}200. On Free Parking, the player does nothing. On Go to Jail, the player moves their token to Jail and does not collect \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}200 if that player passes the GO space. On Just Visiting, the player does nothing. On Chance and Community Chest, the player selects the top card and follows the card's instructions. This paper assumes that, after carrying out the card's instructions, the player will put the card back and shuffle the deck. On Income Tax, the player will pay the bank \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}200 for landing on Income Tax. On Luxury Tax, the player will pay the bank \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}100 for landing on Luxury Tax.
