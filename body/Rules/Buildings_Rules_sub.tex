For streets, completing a color set has its advantages. Once a player acquires all street properties in a color set, the player may buy houses from the bank to increase the rent due to other players. There are a few constraints: a player can have at most 4 houses on one street property at a given moment, and the 4 houses can be upgraded for the cost listed on the street’s deed. Only one hotel is allowed per street property. Additionally, a player has to place houses evenly across streets in that color set. Also, no additional houses can be placed on any street if any street in its color set is mortgaged.

A limited number of houses and hotels are available, so if multiple players want to buy the last house or hotel, it is auctioned to the highest bidder. Once no buildings or hotels remain, players must wait until a player sells theirs back to the bank.
