If a player lands on an unowned property, they can choose to buy it for the listed price. If the player chooses not to buy the property at the listed price, the property goes up for auction. During the auction, bidding starts at \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}10, and the banker sells the property to the highest bidder; all players may bid. The banker gives the deed title to whoever bought the property. If no player bids, then that property remains unowned.
