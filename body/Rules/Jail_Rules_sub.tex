Once a player is sent to jail, they must wait until their next turn, when they have three options to get out. A player can pay \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}50 to the bank, use a Get Out of Jail Free Card if they have one, or try to roll doubles. A player has up to 3 turns to roll doubles. On the third turn, if the player hasn’t rolled doubles, they must pay \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}50 to the bank and use their third turn dice sum to move around the board.
