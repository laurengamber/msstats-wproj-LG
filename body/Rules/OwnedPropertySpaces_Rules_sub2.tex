If a player lands on a street property owned by another player, that player owes the rent listed on the title deed card. The amount owed depends on how many street properties of the same color the owner has and how many houses are built.

If a player lands on a railroad owned by another player, that player owes rent based on how many railroads the owner has. For 1 owned railroad, \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}25 the player owes, for 2 \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}50, for 3 \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}100, and 4 owned railroads \rotatebox[origin=c]{180}{\textwon}200 the player owes.

If a player lands on a utility property owned by another player, the player rolls the dice again; then rent is 4x the sum of the dice roll if 1 utility is owned, or 10x the sum of the dice roll if 2 utilities are owned.

The owner must ask for rent during the player’s turn. If the owner doesn’t ask for rent before the next player rolls, then the player doesn’t have to pay.
